## How to use this

Capture into `raw/` without thinking about structure. Run `/ingest` when you want the
structure to catch up. Run `/tend` when you want the brain to improve itself rather than
grow. The three-way split — capture is cheap, structure is deliberate, curation is
scheduled — is the whole method.
