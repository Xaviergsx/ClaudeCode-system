# 

One-sentence definition. What this is, in plain language, no throat-clearing.

## Why it matters

Two to four sentences: what problem it solves, where it shows up, what breaks without it.

## Detail

The substance. Prose when explaining, bullets only for genuine lists. Name the mechanism,
the numbers, the tradeoff. If you cannot say what this costs, you do not understand it yet.
